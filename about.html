About

The person behind the studio.

Tommy k

Ghana → Australia

Tommy_K Studio is a creative platform for anyone who wants to express their personality through art, fashion and style - without a caption, without explanation, just visuals that speak for themselves.

Born in Ghana, based in Australia - this studio was built for the people who don't have words for thier style, but have everything to show for it.

This is a space for colloboration, creativity and culture. Whether you're an artist, a sneakerhead, or someone who just get dressed differently - you belong here.

What We Showcase

  • Sneaker collections and culture
  • Street style and everyday fits
  • Community submissions - coming soon
